Room X · Estimate, Detect, Commit

The Observer's Ledger

A trading scaffold built on IMM Paper 18: two bounded observers — one on a euro-like tape, one on a gold-like tape — estimate a drifting world, detect the moment it changes, and commit only when both agree, while a third system narrates every decision and pays every cost. This room runs that scaffold, its arithmetic unmodified, on synthetic markets you choose — including a market with no signal at all — and keeps the ledger honestly: friction on every fill, the observer's own anticipated return beside what the tape then did, and the three places where the scaffold's blocks are not coupled the way the paper says a bounded observer's must be.

Instruments, not proofs. Synthetic tapes only — no real prices, no backtest, no edge claim. Paper 18 §11(8): estimation is not edge.

4 · Three couplings that are not there

Paper 18 does not say a bounded observer has three blocks; it says it has three coupled blocks — detection is the "reset pathway" that TI, UE, the sampling rate and the commitment accumulator all consume. Reading the scaffold against the paper, three couplings are missing. Each is a fact about the committed code, measured live on the run above.

4a · The commit threshold never binds.

state.evidence already accumulates return_mean / vol — a sum of standardised drifts. The commit rule then divides it by volatility again (≈ 0.001–0.003) before comparing with a criterion of 2.5–4.5. So the observer acts on any nonzero evidence: on this run the evidence-to-threshold ratio exceeded 1 on … of the bars where the rule was consulted, peaking at …× the threshold. Commit reduces to "trade the sign of the accumulated drift estimate", and the learned criterion has no purchase on it. The switch dimension-corrected variant in §1 compares state.evidence directly to the same threshold — a labelled variant, not the scaffold — and on this run changes ….

The transcript prints the same doubly-normalised number, which is why it says things like "evidence 1680.491 cleared threshold 2.625". By its own anticipated arithmetic, … of the commits on this run expected a negative net return after friction — and were placed anyway, because the rule that commits never reads the number the transcript reports.

4b · The detector cannot see, so nothing ever resets.

An alarm fires when |innovation|·gain exceeds the criterion. But the innovation is standardised by a volatility that is a running average of the true range (about 1.8 σ of the close-to-close move) and that has already absorbed the very bar being judged: a 5 σ jump lands as an innovation of about 1.9, under a criterion of 2.5. On this run the detector fired … times in … bar-steps; across the 24-seed census of §5 the median is … detections per 1,000. Paper 18's whole coupling runs through this alarm — a detected change is what dumps the accumulator ("evidence answers an expired question", R-5), shortens the window (R-1) and raises the update rate (R-2). With the alarm silent, evidence never resets and the observer holds one direction for as long as it took to build it: the longest single-direction run here is …. Drag detection criterion in §1 down to 0.25× and watch the alarms, the resets and the direction changes appear together.

4c · Learning raises a bar the observer never uses.

Feedback moves the criterion up 0.02 after a wrong call and down 0.005 after a right one — break-even at 80 % directional accuracy, so in every world below that the criterion ratchets toward its ceiling (4.5 euro, 5.0 gold). The criterion is read only by the detector (4b), so losing makes alarms rarer, resets rarer, and direction locks longer: the loop is anti-adaptive. On this run learning moved the euro criterion from 2.50 to … and changed … — the switch learning on/off in §1 reruns the identical tape without it.

7 · The fence

Everything here is a finite computation on a synthetic tape. Nothing here is evidence about a market, and a positive number in §1 is not an edge.

No real prices. The scaffold shipped none and this room uses none; the worlds are named generators with seeds. A world in which drift persists at 0.25 σ per bar for 40 bars is a within-regime Sharpe ratio near 4 — the observer is being tested on tapes far kinder than any it would meet.

No backtest. The scaffold's walk_forward.py is committed but never fed real data here; the E-series record of this programme is that structure tests pass and every registered edge test has failed, and that record stands.

Identification, not derivation. Mapping alpha to update elasticity and criterion to a filter threshold is a bridge the scaffold's own README calls "an identification of market features with observer variables, not a proof that markets obey the paper's assumptions". Paper 18's laws are matching laws for a bounded estimator against named drift models (A-OR-3); a market tape is not one of them until shown to be.

What the room does show. A working Estimate block; a Detect block that is blind by construction; a Commit block whose threshold never binds; and a learning loop that pushes the wrong knob — three missing couplings, each measurable, each the kind of thing Paper 18 says a bounded observer cannot afford to miss. That is structure, and structure is what this programme's instruments are for. Repairing the scaffold would be a design; testing the repair would be a registered experiment; neither happens on this page.
